Thanks for using Crash Doctor!

WHAT'S NEW

• Crash guard for bandit factions that have no ship data (e.g. The Old Realms beastmen). It stops crashes after auto-resolved battles and, more importantly, crashes that stopped a save from loading at all. On by default on the "Crash fixes" tab.

• Crash guard for a dying clan being wiped out. On the daily tick, when a clan is removed and one of its parties has lost its internal link, the game crashed; seen with total-conversion mods (e.g. Europe 1100). On by default.

• Crash guard for a siege won by a faction that has no leader. The captured settlement is handed to the hero who actually took it, so the campaign keeps running. On by default.

• Cleanup no longer stops on a single protected folder. "Clear old files from %TEMP%" and the other cleanups used to abort with an "Access denied" error over one locked folder (like Windows' msdtadmin) and delete nothing; now they skip what they can't read and clear the rest.

• New crashes now get a clear verdict: a naval-battle crash from a name-marker mod (Warlord), a crash when loading a save from inside a campaign (a banner/visual mod), and a native crash in Naval DLC ports. These can't be auto-fixed, so Crash Doctor names the cause and tells you what to do.
