Thanks for using Crash Doctor!

WHAT'S NEW

• New guard: a battle-end crash when the party being removed has no clan. The last thing the game does with a party leaving the world is take it off its clan's roll of war parties — and it calls that on the clan without ever checking there is one. A party ends up clanless when a mod destroys or disbands one that was already removed, or clears its clan by hand. The new "Party-removal crash guard (party with no clan)" skips the impossible call and unregisters the party itself, so the removal finishes and the campaign carries on.

• New guard: a deployment-screen crash caused by a banner with no effect. Building the commander's tooltip, the game reads that formation's banner effect without checking it is there — and a banner naming an effect no installed module defines has none, so the battle never starts. The new "Deployment-screen crash guard (banner with broken data)" shows the tooltip without the banner section and logs the banner's id.

• New guard: a crash when a battle ends while one of its sides has lost its leading party. Closing a battle reads that party in half a dozen places in a row and never checks it is still there. The new "Battle-end crash guard (side with no leading party)" applies the game's own rule — the first party still standing on that side takes over — which covers every one of those places at once.

• New guard: a campaign-map crash every few seconds caused by a garrison that lost its settlement. Party healing reads the settlement straight off the party in the garrison branch, so an orphaned garrison brings down the whole campaign tick, seconds after every load. The new "Party-healing crash guard (garrison with no settlement)" skips just that one party's healing for that tick; the orphan is then cleaned up on entering the campaign.

• New guard: a save that dies about a second after every load. Every tick the game asks an ongoing battle whether its sides are at war, without checking the leading party still belongs to a faction — which is exactly what a party destroyed mid-battle looks like, and the broken battle is stored in the save. The new "Battle crash guard (factionless leading party)" ends that battle instead of crashing.

• The save-loading guard for clan strength now works on Bannerlord 1.2 too. That part of the engine is named differently there, so the guard silently never installed — even though the crash is identical.

• New diagnosis: shaders compile but are never saved. The engine writes "Could not write shader cache file" into its log and carries on, so it looks like the build worked and the next launch starts over. Neither a corrupt cache nor the driver — the ProgramData shaders folder is missing or not writable.

FIXES

• Two false "crash prevented" messages removed. One could appear on Bannerlord 1.2 for a crash that cannot happen there at all; the other flagged a faction with no ship data even where the game would have coped. Both sent players off to uninstall an innocent mod.

• The Prevented counter is no longer inflated by a single stuck battle. Where a battle cannot be ended right away, the guard fired on every frame and one event could run the counter into the thousands. It is counted once now.

• One crash, one verdict. A broken-battle crash could show two cards with contradictory advice. Only the card that matches what actually happened is shown.

• The Tune-Up tab speaks Traditional Chinese in the administrator-rights texts too: the hint and the apply/rollback refusals for the pagefile, the TDR delay and Game DVR were shown in Simplified Chinese.

• The Saves tab no longer comes up empty because of one unreadable subfolder inside Game Saves. Such a folder is skipped instead of aborting the whole scan.
